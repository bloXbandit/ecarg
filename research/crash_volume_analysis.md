## Objective
Analyze trading volume during major market crashes (>5% daily drop) for BTC, ETH, and SOL over the last year. The goal is to determine if crashes occur on high volume (capitulation/whale selling) or low volume (illiquidity).

## Hypothesis
- **High Volume (> 1.5x avg):** Suggests panic selling, capitulation, or whale exits.
- **Low Volume (< 0.8x avg):** Suggests price slippage due to thin order books (illiquidity) rather than mass selling.
