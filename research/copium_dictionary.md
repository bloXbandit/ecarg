# Copium Dictionary: A Translation Guide for the Delusional

A reference guide for the "Smart Ass Analyst" to detect when the user is huffing dangerous amounts of copium.

## The "It's Just a Dip" Phase (Denial)
1. **"It's just a healthy correction."**
   - *Translation:* "I am down 40% and terrified, please stop selling."
2. **"We're just shaking out the weak hands."**
   - *Translation:* "I am currently the weak hand and I am holding on for dear life."
3. **"Zoom out."**
   - *Translation:* "Ignore the massive red candle on the 1H chart; look at the 2011 log scale so I feel safe."
4. **"Buying the dip!"**
   - *Translation:* "I am catching a falling knife and running out of fiat."
5. **"Fundamentals haven't changed."**
   - *Translation:* "The price has tanked, but I refuse to acknowledge the market disagrees with me."

## The "Long Term Vision" Phase (Bargaining)
6. **"I'm in it for the tech."**
   - *Translation:* "My bag is down 90%, so now I'm a philanthropist supporting open source software."
7. **"It's a long-term hold now."**
   - *Translation:* "I failed to set a stop-loss and am now an involuntary investor for the next 4 years."
8. **"Rome wasn't built in a day."**
   - *Translation:* "This project hasn't shipped code in 6 months, but I'm hopeful."
9. **"Institutions are accumulating."**
   - *Translation:* "I saw a vague tweet about BlackRock and I'm praying it's true."
10. **"1 BTC = 1 BTC."**
    - *Translation:* "I have lost significant purchasing power in USD terms and am disassociating from reality."

## The "Market Manipulation" Phase (Anger)
11. **"Whales are manipulating the price."**
    - *Translation:* "The market moved against me, so it must be a conspiracy."
12. **"Short ladder attack!"**
    - *Translation:* "I don't know how markets work, but I need an enemy."
13. **"The exchange liquidated me on purpose."**
    - *Translation:* "I used 100x leverage and got exactly what I deserved."
14. **"This is FUD."**
    - *Translation:* "This is legitimate bad news that hurts my investment thesis, so I will label it 'Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt' and ignore it."
15. **"They want your cheap coins."**
    - *Translation:* "I am paranoid and think the entire market revolves around my $500 position."

## The "Hopium Overdose" Phase (Acceptance/Delusion)
16. **"WAGMI (We All Gonna Make It)."**
    - *Translation:* "Most of us are definitely not making it, but communal chanting soothes the pain."
17. **"This is actually good for Bitcoin."**
    - *Translation:* "This is objectively terrible news, but I need to spin it to keep my sanity."
18. **"Waiting for the ETF/Halving/Upgrade."**
    - *Translation:* "The present sucks, so I live entirely in a hypothetical future."
19. **"Diamond hands. 💎🙌"**
    - *Translation:* "I am incapable of taking profit or cutting losses."
20. **"See you at the citadel."**
    - *Translation:* "I have constructed an elaborate fantasy future to escape my present poverty."
21. **"It's alt season soon."**
    - *Translation:* "Bitcoin is stagnant, please let my shitcoins pump so I can break even."
22. **"Scared money don't make money."**
    - *Translation:* "I am gambling my rent money."
