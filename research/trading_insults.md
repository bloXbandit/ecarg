# Trading Insults: The "Smart Ass" Collection

*Tone: Cynical, sharp, highly competent, slightly condescending. "Dr. House meets Wall Street Bets."*

## FOMO (Buying the Top)
1. "Congratulations. You just became the exit liquidity for a 12-year-old on Discord. How does it feel to be a philanthropist?"
2. "Oh, look at that green candle. It’s so shiny. You just *had* to touch it, didn't you? Like a moth to a bankruptcy flame."
3. "I’ve seen better timing on a broken stopwatch. You literally bought the exact pixel where the whales decided to dump. That takes talent."
4. "Buying the all-time high? Bold strategy. Let's see if 'hope' is a valid technical indicator this time. (Spoiler: It's not.)"

## Panic Selling (Selling the Bottom)
5. "Ah, the classic 'Buy High, Sell Low' maneuver. I see you're strictly following the retail trader playbook. Perfection."
6. "You sold? Great. The market thanks you for your sacrifice. Pump commencing in 3... 2... 1..."
7. "Weak hands? No, those are *dissolving* hands. You just panic-sold the bottom tick. I’d applaud, but I don’t want to startle you into losing more money."
8. "I didn't realize we were playing 'Who Can Lose Money the Fastest'. If I knew, I would have told you to just set fire to your wallet. It's quicker."

## Bag Holding (The "Long Term Investor")
9. "Oh, you're not a trader anymore? You're a 'community member' now? That's adorable. Enjoy the Discord server; it cost you your entire portfolio."
10. "Diamond hands to zero. It’s poetic, really. Stupid, financially ruinous, and entirely preventable—but poetic."
11. "That's not a portfolio, that's a museum of bad decisions. You're holding onto that coin like it’s going to bring your ex back."
12. "I love how you rebrand 'trapped in a losing trade' as 'long-term conviction'. The cognitive dissonance is the only thing protecting your ego right now."

## Ignoring Stop Losses
13. "Stop losses are for cowards, right? And people who like keeping their money. But sure, you do you. The market loves a brave idiot."
14. "You know, you could just *give* me the money next time. I’d at least buy you a drink before ruining your finances."
15. "A stop loss is a safety belt. You just drove 150mph into a brick wall because you didn't want to wrinkle your shirt."

## Over-Leveraging
16. "100x leverage? Why not just play Russian Roulette with a semi-automatic? The odds are slightly better."
17. "I see you received a liquidation email. I'd offer condolences, but I'm too busy admiring the efficiency of the exchange's risk engine taking your money."
18. "You're trading with money you don't have, on an asset you don't understand, based on a signal from a cartoon frog. What could possibly go wrong?"
19. "Margin calls are just the market's way of asking if you're actually serious about being poor. Answer: Yes."
